# Plans

Index of open plans. Each active plan gets a file in
`.agents/plans/<name>.md`; only the line pointing to it remains here, with its
status and executor.

Completed plans are removed from here and their files are discarded—the plan
describes what to do, and once completed it describes the past. Anything worth
preserving becomes a memory in `.agents/memories/`.
